Pokémon Rifts is a Pokémon fangame developed in RPG Maker XP using Pokémon Essentials v21.1 and various community resources. Full credits available in the Google Drive download folder and bundled with the game. Play-Faedos is a modded Pokémon Showdown website loaded with the mechanics and Faedes from Rifts. Pokémon Rifts and Faedes developed by Technovoid. Play-Faedos server developed by Hamsterwings. Faedos landing page by Vlinx. Faede art commissioned from j.leonart.
