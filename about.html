Pokémon Rifts is a Pokémon fangame developed in RPG Maker XP using Pokémon Essentials v21.1 and various community resources. Full credits available in the Google Drive download folder and bundled with the game.

Play-Faedos is a modded Pokémon Showdown website loaded with the mechanics and Faedes from Rifts.
